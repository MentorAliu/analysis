Feature research and recommendations — 5 September 2026

Status: research and proposed product decisions. This document does not authorize implementation or modify the active execution plan. The repository remains a documentation baseline. Recommendations are reasoned inferences from the cited evidence, not measured customer demand, investment-performance claims, or proven commercial advantages.

**I recommend a saved research-thesis monitor as the next customer-facing feature, supported by a forward signal record and precise explanations of changes.** Token economics comparisons and supply-event monitoring are useful later candidates, subject to licensed coverage. A standalone AI brief, generic alerts, or an unlock calendar would add capabilities but provide weak differentiation from the products examined.

The evidence supports specific problems: historical data can change, notifications can disrupt attention, protocol revenue differs from tokenholder value, and an apparently exact unlock date can have coarse underlying precision. It does not establish that customers will choose this application or pay a particular price. I found no basis for claiming an uncontested feature gap or guaranteed income opportunity.

The review used the repository's [product specification](../product/product-spec.md), [scoring model](../design/scoring-model.md), and [provider requirements](../engineering/data-sources.md), current official product/API documentation, original academic research, and a vendor's worked research example. Public forum searches surfaced substantial builder promotion and unverifiable performance claims; those were not treated as customer-demand evidence. Paid competitor accounts, interviews, live provider payloads and independent benchmark reproduction were outside this review.

**Current competitors narrow the opportunity.** Messari documents watchlist summaries across research, governance, news and events, plus grouped-condition alerts. TradingView documents multi-condition alerts and watchlist alerts. Consequently, my earlier suggestion of a watchlist brief needs a sharper purpose; a digest alone is already a competitive offering. [Messari watchlists](https://docs.messari.io/user-guides/messari-features/watchlists), [Messari Alert Manager](https://docs.messari.io/user-guides/tools/alert-manager), [TradingView multi-condition alerts](https://www.tradingview.com/blog/en/multi-condition-alerts-54525/), [TradingView watchlist alerts](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000739708-watchlist-alerts-your-trading-edge/).

DefiLlama already exposes token economic rights, fee-switch status and value-accrual mechanisms. Token Terminal presents standardized financial statements. Tokenomist has tokenomics updates and unlock research. These discoveries rule out positioning those basic views as inventions or established competitive advantages. [DefiLlama Token Rights](https://defillama.com/token-rights), [Token Terminal](https://tokenterminal.com/), [Tokenomist Updates Panel](https://docs.tokenomist.ai/updates-panel).

The order above is a product judgment based on fit and dependencies. It is not a fabricated numerical opportunity score.

**A saved thesis monitor would preserve why the user is watching an asset.** A watchlist typically records the asset; this proposed feature would also record the research horizon, supporting observations, opposing observations, specific conditions that would require a review, and the evidence snapshot at creation. Free text remains the user's interpretation. Machine-evaluated conditions must use explicit structured features or validated events.

Illustrative behavior, using hypothetical inputs: a user records that an asset's relative strength is improving while derivatives crowding remains within a chosen condition. Later, the application reports which condition changed, the previous and current values, when each input was observed, and whether a missing input prevents evaluation. The message should say that a research condition failed or needs review; it should not assert that the investment thesis has been objectively disproved or direct a trade.

Useful states include condition met, condition not met, conflicting evidence, and not evaluable. The last state matters: a stale funding feed must not masquerade as a deterioration in market evidence. User-authored notes and condition changes need version history so later review can distinguish a changed thesis from a changed market.

The notification mechanism should detect relevant state transitions, deduplicate repeated triggers, allow user-controlled scheduling and preserve critical-event exceptions. A 2015 controlled study found that phone notifications could disrupt an attention task even without phone interaction. That supports restraint in notification design, but does not prove that fewer alerts improve crypto research or that delayed alerts are appropriate for every user. [Original study abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26121498/).

The potential commercial value is continuity: users can resume their own reasoning, see whether its conditions remain satisfied, and revisit the decision later. That is a retention hypothesis. Messari and TradingView already cover important components; this app would need to demonstrate that the complete workflow is preferable, not merely assemble more configurable alerts.

The smallest implementation should use already-approved price, relative-strength and derivatives features, one saved hypothesis per research item, explicit conditions, one notification channel and a timestamped review history. Automatic interpretation of arbitrary news, unlimited rule composition, and an LLM agent are unnecessary for the first experiment. This would require a subsequent execution plan because watchlists and alerts are excluded from the current technical slice.

Validation should compare the user's existing workflow with the prototype on matched research cases, including a meaningful deterioration, a harmless fluctuation and missing data. Measure time to a correct review, missed important changes, unnecessary interruptions, and repeated use. Faster completion accompanied by more errors is not a success. Paid renewal after using the workflow is needed before claiming commercial value.

**A forward signal record would make the app accountable for its original output.** Glassnode's March 2026 worked example reruns the same illustrative strategy with revised versus point-in-time exchange-balance data and obtains materially different results. This demonstrates a real data-integrity failure mode. It is a vendor demonstration, not an independently reproduced result or proof about this repository's unbuilt model. [Glassnode's worked example](https://research.glassnode.com/why-use-point-in-time-data/).

Every published ranking should retain its issued time, logical as-of time, model version, exact feature inputs, eligible universe, data-quality state and reference price convention. The display should attach outcomes after a prespecified horizon elapses, show pending/incomplete records, and preserve poor outcomes. Separate originally issued observations from reconstructed historical research. An observation loaded today cannot be presented as a signal the application issued last year.

Users should be able to inspect a signal's subsequent return, its benchmark-relative result, and the intervening adverse/favorable price path under explicit definitions. Aggregates should show sample sizes, periods, coverage and model versions. A positive absolute return is insufficient to establish a useful ranking when the whole market rose. A simple market benchmark and a simple relative-strength ordering are appropriate comparisons to investigate; benchmark definitions must be frozen before evaluation.

Academic work by Liu, Tsyvinski and Wu identified market, size and momentum factors in its historical cryptocurrency sample. This supports checking whether apparent usefulness is simply common exposure. It does not establish that the same factor model will explain the current market or prescribe weights for this app. [Original working paper and published-version reference](https://www.nber.org/papers/w25882).

Research outputs remain observational. They should not be labeled realized trading profits. Multiple signals on the same asset and overlapping return horizons are not independent evidence. Avoid selecting whichever horizon or subperiod looks best after seeing outcomes. If there are too few cases, show that limitation instead of a dramatic percentage.

The commercial hypothesis is that inspectable history reduces the amount of trust a prospective customer must grant an unknown ranking provider. Basic accuracy and performance disclosure should remain accessible; customers might later pay for advanced comparisons and their own research history. No tested conversion uplift or price premium is established.

Start collection with the existing three-asset universe. That can verify the recording and outcome pipeline, but cannot establish broad cross-sectional ranking skill. Publication timestamps, customer-held exports, or an independently timestamped digest can strengthen auditability. An append-only database or a self-controlled hash chain alone does not prove that the operator never rewrote all history; do not advertise independent verification without an independent mechanism.

**Change explanations need to distinguish different reasons for movement.** This is a direct deduction from the repo's cross-sectional normalization, missing-data policies and versioned models. An asset can rise in rank because its peers fell even when its own inputs did not improve. Its composite can also change when the peer set used for normalization changes. Treating either event as new bullish evidence would be misleading.

For a before/after comparison, show asset-input changes, changes in comparison assets/universe, model-version changes, and coverage/freshness changes separately. Preserve the comparison snapshot IDs. If a nonlinear regime adjustment or interaction prevents a simple additive decomposition, disclose the method and residual instead of inventing exact feature contributions.

This belongs inside the asset detail and thesis review, rather than as another dashboard. Explain enough for the user to determine whether the change warrants investigation. It primarily protects product trust and research accuracy; I would not sell basic correctness as an optional premium add-on.

Validation cases should include unchanged asset inputs with falling peers, a new peer entering the universe, a model revision, a stale derivatives input, and a true feature change. The application must identify the correct class of change without unsupported market-causality language.

**Token economics comparisons could answer whether protocol activity reaches the token holder.** DefiLlama distinguishes fees, protocol revenue and holders revenue, and separately explains that USD TVL can change because the underlying assets changed price. Those are materially different measurements. A high usage or TVL number alone does not establish tokenholder value capture. [DefiLlama definitions](https://defillama.com/data-definitions).

The proposed comparison would place comparable protocols and time periods side by side, separating fees paid by users, revenue retained by the protocol, realized holder distributions, executed buybacks, burns, incentives, issuance and scheduled releases. It should also identify who is eligible for a distribution and what mechanism is active. Announced, governance-approved and executed mechanisms should remain separate states linked to their sources.

Do not sum buyback spending, the resulting burn and a vendor's holder-revenue aggregate if they describe the same economic flow. Do not treat circulating-supply releases as newly minted total supply. Distinguish treasury-funded purchases from purchases funded by ongoing revenue where the source supports that classification; otherwise show unknown. Use separate measurement periods and precise units instead of annualizing a single exceptional day into a stable yield.

A comparison must respect applicability. Bitcoin, a general-purpose blockchain, and a DeFi application cannot be forced into an identical income statement. Protocol identity also needs a documented relationship to token identity: one protocol can involve several tokens and different economic rights. This extends the canonical-identity model and requires an explicit design decision.

The product opportunity is to help users compare and monitor economic mechanisms within their existing research workflow. DefiLlama's Token Rights and Token Terminal's statements are direct competitive alternatives, so a duplicate table is insufficient. A pilot should test whether users can correctly explain the path from activity to token economics, and whether monitoring changes to that path provides repeated value. [DefiLlama Token Rights](https://defillama.com/token-rights), [Token Terminal](https://tokenterminal.com/).

Data coverage, commercial rights and maintenance burden are not yet established for the repo. Begin with a small set of appropriately comparable, licensed examples before promising universal coverage or allowing these fields to influence the composite. This is a later expansion, not a silent enlargement of the current slice.

**Supply-event monitoring should communicate uncertainty and revisions.** Tokenomist's current v5 unlock example includes source, last update, allocation, reference-price timing and an unlock precision that may be month-level. Its separate daily-emission endpoint covers a different data surface. Therefore, a precise countdown cannot always be justified from the apparent timestamp alone. [Unlock Events v5](https://docs.tokenomist.ai/api-documents/unlock-events/v5), [Daily Emission v5](https://docs.tokenomist.ai/api-documents/daily-emission/v5).

For watched assets, show the next scheduled event, date precision, recipient category, the defined supply denominator and what changed from the previously recorded schedule. Preserve original and revised schedules. Distinguish a scheduled release, an observed claim or transfer, newly issued tokens, and a confirmed burn. A transfer to an exchange is not proof that tokens were sold.

Supply context may include size relative to a defined circulating supply and licensed liquidity observations, but these ratios are context, not predicted price impact. Report the timestamps and source coverage used for each denominator. Do not infer an inevitable sell-off or a short recommendation from an unlock.

Tokenomist already provides an Updates Panel, so this is a useful integration candidate rather than a demonstrated market gap. Its public commercial-rights matrix marks redistribution rights for Enterprise, not the ordinary API tiers. Exact contract and coverage validation would precede implementation. [Updates Panel](https://docs.tokenomist.ai/updates-panel), [API plans and rights](https://docs.tokenomist.ai/api-documents/introduction).

I also reviewed Keyrock's unlock study. I would not turn its widely repeated percentage claim into this app's prediction rule: the public article is not sufficient to establish an independently reproduced, causal, out-of-sample predictor for the app's universe. Its observations motivate investigation, not certainty. [Original Keyrock study](https://keyrock.com/from-locked-to-liquidity-what-16000-token-unlocks-teach-us/).

**The next product decision should be narrow and testable.** Complete the existing technical slice within its authorized boundaries, preserving enough issued history to support future outcomes. Then propose a subsequent execution plan for one saved research item, evidence-state transitions, precise change explanations and a basic forward record. Add token economics or supply-event monitoring only when the selected customer's task requires them and the data can be used commercially.

I would defer a generic chat interface, an enlarged indicator library, a standalone correlation heatmap and a standalone unlock calendar. Current competitors already cover much of that territory; none of the sources reviewed establishes that another copy would persuade customers to switch. The research supports building a coherent process from observed evidence to a saved hypothesis to monitored changes to later review. Whether customers prefer and pay for that process remains the experiment to run.

Research limitations: documentation establishes advertised capabilities, not paid-feature quality or customer satisfaction. Academic results are bounded by their samples and tasks. API examples establish documented structure, not complete coverage or licensing approval. No customer interviews, paid pilots, market-data requests, scoring experiments, provider purchases, or application implementation were conducted for this report.
